# Business Insights
##Summary

This analysis examines the used smartphone market by evaluating selling prices across different brands, device conditions, storage capacities, RAM sizes, and phone ages. The dashboard helps identify the main factors that influence resale value and supports data-driven pricing decisions.

##Key Findings
###Brand Performance
OnePlus recorded the highest average selling price, followed closely by Apple. This indicates that premium smartphone brands retain their resale value better than other brands.
###Device Condition
Phones in Like New condition achieved the highest average selling price. As the device condition declined from Excellent to Good and Fair, the average resale value decreased significantly.
###Storage Capacity
Devices with larger storage capacities generally sold at higher prices. Models with 256 GB and 512 GB storage consistently achieved better resale values than lower-capacity models.
###RAM Impact
The relationship between RAM and selling price appears relatively weak. Devices with similar RAM configurations can have noticeably different prices depending on other factors such as brand and condition.
###Phone Age
Older phones generally have lower selling prices. However, the relationship is not perfectly linear, suggesting that brand reputation and device condition also play important roles in determining resale value.

#Recommendation

Prioritize inventory from premium brands such as OnePlus and Apple, as they maintain higher resale values. Encourage sellers to keep devices in Like New condition before resale, since condition has a significant impact on selling price. Higher-storage models should also receive greater attention because they tend to command better prices. Finally, pricing decisions should consider multiple factors—including brand, condition, storage capacity, and phone age—rather than relying on a single specification such as RAM.
